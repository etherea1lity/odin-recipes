Mac and Cheese

Mac and cheese is a comfort food classic that features pasta tossed in a creamy cheese sauce. It's easy to make and always a hit!

Ingredients

  • 8 oz elbow macaroni
  • 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Instructions

  1. Cook the macaroni according to the package instructions.
  2. In a saucepan, melt the butter and whisk in the flour.
  3. Gradually add the milk, stirring constantly until smooth.
  4. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce heat and stir in the cheese until melted.
  5. Season with salt and pepper.
  6. Combine the cheese sauce with the cooked macaroni and serve hot.
